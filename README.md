# TEG E-Scooter - Waste Heat to Free Range | Dexathon 2026

### Problem Statement
In E-scooters, the BLDC motor generates 60-70°C of waste heat which is completely wasted.

### Our Solution
We use a Thermoelectric Generator (TEG) TEC1-12706 to convert waste heat into free electricity using the Seebeck Effect.

### Working Principle
TEG Module (2V) -> MT3608 Boost Converter -> 5V Regulated -> E-Scooter Battery

### Impact & Benefits
- Free Range: 360m extra per day
- Cost: < Rs. 1000, Lifespan: 10-15 Years
- Environmental: Zero pollution, Energy recycling

### Tech Stack
- Hardware: TEG TEC1-12706, MT3608 Boost Converter
