# TEG E-Scooter - Waste Heat to Free Range | Dexathon 2026

**Team:** mswetha837-ux | **Theme:** Sustainable Energy Recovery

### 1. Problem Statement
E-scooter BLDC motor la 60-70°C waste heat waste aaguthu. Range anxiety irukku, battery waste aaguthu.

### 2. Our Solution
Thermoelectric Generator (TEG) TEC1-12706 use panni waste heat-a free electricity-a maathurom.
Motor meedhu TEG vaithu, heat nala current generate pannurom.

### 3. How it Works
TEG Module (2V) -> MT3608 Boost Converter -> 5V Regulated Output -> E-Scooter Battery (Trickle Charging)
Seebeck Effect Principle: Heat difference = Electricity

### 4. Impact & Benefits
- **Free Range:** 360 meters extra per day
- **Cost:** < Rs. 1000 (Low cost kit)
- **Life:** 10-15 Years (No maintenance)
- **Environmental:** Zero pollution, energy recycling
- **Economic:** Save money for students & delivery partners
